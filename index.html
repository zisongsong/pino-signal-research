AI assists the search. Sources carry the claim.

Software can accelerate document discovery and organization. It cannot turn an unsupported price estimate into evidence.

Search order

Executed contracts, amendments, awards, board packets, RFP responses, public proposals, customer agreements and archives.

Quoted values

Reported exactly as stated in the original record, with date, scope and covered population when available.

Derived values

Kept separate from vendor quotes and accompanied by the full formula and input assumptions.

Missing evidence

Reported as partial or not found. Search snippets, unsourced blogs and AI summaries do not support a pricing claim.
