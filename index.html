Questions

Do you use private customer data?

No. The standard pack uses public pages and public conversations. Private support tickets, call transcripts or customer interviews are used only when you supply them with permission.

Is the research AI-assisted?

Yes. AI speeds up collection, clustering and drafting. Every delivered claim is checked against its linked source, and generic unmodified output is not included.

Which products are a good fit?

B2B SaaS, AI tools and digital products with an identifiable buyer and enough public discussion to study. Products with no users, no category conversations and no clear buyer may not have enough evidence for a useful pack.

What happens if public evidence is too thin?

We tell you before production begins. You can choose a refund or a smaller competitor-language pack using the evidence that exists.
