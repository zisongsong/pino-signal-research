Research standard

Original records first

Contracts, amendments, awards, board packets, RFP responses, public proposals, customer agreements and archives take priority over secondary summaries.

Evidence types stay separate

Quoted prices, calculated values and authorization ceilings are labeled so a decision-maker can see what the source actually proves.

Every calculation is reproducible

Derived values retain the original inputs and formula. Hidden assumptions are not converted into false precision.

Missing data remains missing

An unavailable population or unit price is reported as an evidence gap. It is not filled with an AI estimate.
