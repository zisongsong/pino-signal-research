# Pino Pricing Intelligence

Public-source research for negotiated B2B markets.

The product traces hard-to-find prices to contracts, procurement records, RFPs, customer proposals, board packets, and public archives. Every material finding records the source, date, scope, evidence type, calculation when derived, caveat, and confidence rating.

- [Product page](https://zisongsong.github.io/pino-signal-research/)
- [Employee-benefits pricing sample](https://zisongsong.github.io/pino-signal-research/sample.html)

## Paid pilot

- Three named competitors.
- Public-document research only.
- Up to two material findings per competitor.
- English PDF plus CSV evidence ledger.
- Three-business-day delivery.
- One factual correction.
- Fixed price: $149.

Missing public evidence is reported as missing. AI-generated prices and unsupported estimates are excluded.
